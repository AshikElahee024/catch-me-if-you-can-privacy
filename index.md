# Privacy Policy

**Catch Me If You Can**

Last updated: November 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Catch Me If You Can ("the App") handles information when you use the game.

The App is a multiplayer party card game designed for players to enjoy together on a device.

Developer: Ashik

Contact: elaheeashik3660@gmail.com

## 2. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play.

The App does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

Players may enter names during gameplay. These names and game scores are used to operate the game. They are not sent to the developer, assuming the App operates entirely offline and does not use data-collecting services.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

The App may use information entered during gameplay, such as player names and scores, to provide its game features.

Any game information stored locally remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

You can remove locally stored game information by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App, subject to how the App stores its data.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services to collect or share personal information, assuming no advertising, analytics, or other data-collecting SDKs are included.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the information they collect and how it is used.

## 5. Data Security

Because the App is designed to operate offline and does not transmit personal information to the developer, the developer does not maintain a server containing player information.

Please keep your device secure to protect any information stored locally.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to the developer through the App, please contact the developer using the email address below.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change.

The updated policy will be published at the same privacy policy URL, with a revised "Last updated" date.
